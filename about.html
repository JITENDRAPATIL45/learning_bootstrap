About Us

Patil Dealership is a multi-brand automobile dealership focused on bringing customers a diverse selection of vehicles under one roof. From practical everyday cars to premium and high-performance automobiles, we aim to make finding the right vehicle simple, transparent, and convenient.

Our collection features vehicles across different brands and segments, including BMW, Mercedes-Benz, Tata, Bugatti, and other leading automotive brands.

We believe buying a car should be more than a transaction. It should be a smooth experience built around choice, trust, transparency, and customer satisfaction.

mission

To provide customers with a reliable, convenient, and transparent car-buying experience by offering a wide range of vehicles, professional assistance, and customer-focused services.

Our Vission

To become a trusted destination for multi-brand automobiles, recognized for exceptional customer experience, quality vehicles, and professional service.

Our History:

From Automobile Passion to Customer-Focused Dealership:

Patil Dealership was established with a simple idea: give customers more choice when searching for their next car. Starting with a focus on providing customers with quality vehicles and dependable service, the dealership has grown toward a multi-brand automotive model, bringing different vehicle categories and brands together in one platform.

Today, Patil Dealership aims to serve customers looking for everything from everyday vehicles to premium and high-performance automobiles, while maintaining a strong focus on transparency and customer experience.

Our Journey

The Beginning

Patil Dealership begins with a vision of creating a customer-focused automobile business in 2005.

Growing the Collection

The dealership expands its vehicle selection across multiple brands and segments.

Multi-Brand Experience

Customers gain access to a broader range of vehicles from popular and premium brands.

Digital Transformation

Patil Dealership brings its vehicle collection and customer experience online, making it easier for customers to explore cars and connect with the dealership.

Welcome to Patil Dealership, your trusted destination for quality vehicles since 1989.
